Good news from the research side. For the third consecutive year, I have been included in the Stanford/[Elsevier](https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/datasets/btchxktzyw/9) list of the world's top 2% of scientists.

The ranking is based on standardized citation indicators, with the latest update covering citation data through 2025. It is encouraging to see that our research on energy systems modelling continues to gain visibility and contribute to the field.

Many thanks to all my colleagues and collaborators who have contributed to this work over the years. This recognition also reflects our joint efforts.
