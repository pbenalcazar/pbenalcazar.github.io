I am grateful to have been selected as the candidate for Vice President, Membership & Affiliate Relations in the 2026 IAEE elections. As an IAEE member since 2017 and a former Student Representative, I have experienced the value of the Association’s international community and its network of affiliates. This role offers an opportunity to support stronger connections among members, affiliates, and early-career professionals. I thank the IAEE Council for its trust and for the opportunity to contribute further to the Association. Further information is available on the [IAEE Elections 2026 page](https://iaee.org/publications/iaee-elections-2026).
