Our paper "Reinforcement Learning and Rule-Based Peer-to-Peer Pricing in Residential PV-BES Communities" has been accepted at the International Conference on Advanced Research in Technologies, Information, Innovation and Sustainability (ARTIIS 2026). The conference takes place at Universidade Europeia in Lisbon, Portugal, on 11 to 13 November and 16 November 2026.

The paper was developed within the [CoEnerBuild](/projects/coenerbuild/) project. It compares reinforcement learning and rule-based approaches to pricing peer-to-peer energy trading in residential communities with photovoltaic (PV) and battery energy storage (BES) systems.
