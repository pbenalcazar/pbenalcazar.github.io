Our paper, [“A Model-Based Approach to Assessing Operational and Cost Performance of Hydrogen, Battery, and EV Storage in Community Energy Systems”](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/19/3/794), has been published in *Energies*. The study compares hydrogen, battery, and electric-vehicle storage in a Polish community energy system, focusing on operating costs, self-sufficiency, self-consumption, and interaction with the electricity grid.
