Our paper, [“An integrated framework for the optimal expansion of hydro-dependent power systems under water-resource uncertainty”](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590174525004295), has been published in *Energy Conversion and Management: X*. The study presents a long-term generation and transmission planning framework that combines hydrological uncertainty, reservoir operation, and grid expansion, with an application to Ecuador’s power system.
